# Ripple & More Brand Strategy Study

## Brand Overview

**Ripple & More Limited** — "Building Digital Wealth"

**Tagline:** One company. Multiple ventures. One mission.

**Mission:** From crypto education to AI automation, e-commerce, branding, and automotive — built for Africa.

---

## Service Portfolio

### 1. AI Customer Agent 🤖
- **What:** 24/7 AI employee for WhatsApp/Telegram/Web
- **Price:** $500 setup + $99-499/month
- **Target:** Businesses needing customer support
- **Markets:** Ghana, Nigeria, Dubai, Global

### 2. Fast Web Apps ⚡
- **What:** Full-stack apps deployed in 24 hours
- **Price:** $200-1000
- **Target:** Startups, businesses needing MVPs
- **Stack:** React, Node.js, Docker

### 3. Workflow Automation 🔄
- **What:** n8n/Make.com automation
- **Price:** $300-1000 setup
- **Target:** Businesses with repetitive tasks
- **ROI:** Setup cost recovered in first month

### 4. TechAfrik 📱
- **What:** AI & Blockchain media platform
- **Platforms:** Telegram, WhatsApp, TikTok, IG, LI, X
- **Audience:** 10,000+ African tech enthusiasts
- **Content:** Daily crypto/AI education

### 5. Dobuygoods 🛒
- **What:** Crypto marketplace for used goods
- **Payment:** Bitcoin, USDT
- **Focus:** Ghana & West Africa
- **Problem:** Spending crypto locally

### 6. SignupGhana 🎨
- **What:** Branding & visual marketing
- **Services:** 3D signage, LED screens, merchandise
- **Market:** Ghana businesses
- **Edge:** Creative + fabrication tech

### 7. Biskaken Auto 🔧
- **What:** Automotive repair + crypto payments
- **Services:** Engine, brakes, electrical, body work
- **Unique:** Accepts crypto for auto services
- **Market:** Ghana vehicle owners

### 8. ResearchClaw 🧠
- **What:** AI engine powering all ventures
- **Services:** Agentic AI, social automation, workflows
- **Internal:** Powers Ripple & More ecosystem

## Brand Positioning

### Target Markets (Priority)

| Market | Priority | Strategy |
|--------|----------|----------|
| **Ghana** | #1 | Home market, crypto adoption, local trust |
| **Dubai** | #2 | Business hub, crypto-friendly, expat Africans |
| **Nigeria** | #3 | Largest African market, tech-savvy youth |
| **USA** | #4 | Diaspora, high purchasing power, referrals |
| **Global** | #5 | Remote services, crypto universal |

### Value Propositions by Market

**Ghana:**
- "Built by Ghanaians for Ghanaians"
- Local trust + global tech
- Crypto payments accepted
- Physical presence (Biskaken, SignupGhana)

**Dubai:**
- "Africa meets UAE"
- Business hub gateway
- Crypto-first services
- Professional, premium positioning

**Nigeria:**
- "Scale with Africa's largest market"
- High-volume solutions
- Youth-focused (TikTok, Instagram)
- Entrepreneur empowerment

**USA:**
- "Connect with African innovation"
- Diaspora investment opportunities
- Remote collaboration
- Premium consulting
